We joined RQ 2026, the 18th International Conference on Rapidly Quenched and Metastable Materials, held in Busan from August 23 to 28.

Prof. Kook Noh Yoon served on the Local Organizing Committee and presented *"Evaluation of Self-ion Irradiation-Induced Hardening in F/M Steels and the Hardness Correction Using Pile-Up Measurements"*.
