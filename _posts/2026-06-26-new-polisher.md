Our first major piece of equipment has arrived: a twin-wheel automatic grinder/polisher (TOPTECH), now installed in our lab (Room 415, Bldg. N8).

![Auto polisher in the ASTRO Lab](/assets/img/blog/2026-06-polisher.jpg){: .img-fluid .rounded .z-depth-1}

A good polish is where every microstructure analysis starts. With this machine we can now prepare metallographic samples in-house.
