Team ASTRO attended the 2026 Spring Meeting of the Korean Institute of Metals and Materials (KIM), held in Jeju from April 28 to 30.

Prof. Kook Noh Yoon gave an invited talk in the Phase Transformation session, *"Self-Constructed Bi-continuous RHEA-Cu Composites via LMD process for Nuclear Reactors"*, on why next-generation fusion and fission reactors need materials that withstand extreme environments, and how liquid metal dealloying can build them.
