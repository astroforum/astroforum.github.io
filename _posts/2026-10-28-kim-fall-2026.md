Team ASTRO attended the 2026 Fall Meeting of the Korean Institute of Metals and Materials (KIM).

Prof. Kook Noh Yoon gave an invited talk in the Phase Transformation session on October 28, *"Tailoring Deformation Mechanisms in Ti-Zr-Hf HCP Multicomponent Alloys via Crystallographic Control"*, based on our recent *Acta Materialia* paper on composition–microstructure–property relationships in the HCP Ti-Zr-Hf system.

![Invited talk at the 2026 KIM Fall Meeting](/assets/img/blog/2026-10-kim-fall-talk2.jpg){: .img-fluid .rounded .z-depth-1}

Our capstone design students also presented their first poster in the undergraduate student session (P1-18): *"Microstructure and mechanical properties of Cr-Mo-W-based multicomponent alloys with Ni addition."* Over two semesters, they designed the alloys with CALPHAD, made them by arc melting, and worked on improving strength and oxidation resistance. Great work, everyone!
