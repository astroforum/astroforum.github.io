On March 1, 2026, Prof. Kook Noh Yoon joined the Department of Materials Science and Engineering at Hanbat National University as an assistant professor, and ASTRO Lab (**A**lloy design for **ST**ructural **RO**bustness) opened its doors.

![Hanbat National University campus](/assets/img/blog/2026-03-hanbat-campus.jpg){: .img-fluid .rounded .z-depth-1}

We design metallic alloys that stay strong and reliable in extreme environments. If you are interested in joining us, feel free to knock on the door of Room 510, Bldg. N8.
